# Public-domain edition sources

Parallel’s bundled classics are processed into aligned bilingual reading passages from editions that identify the underlying works and translations as public domain. The app removes source-site headers, navigation, formatting, and license boilerplate from the reading text; it retains edition acknowledgements and links back to each source page.

## Russian editions

- *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland*: A. Rozhdestvenskaya’s 1909 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Приключения_Алисы_в_стране_чудес_(Кэрролл;_Рождественская)); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 11](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11).
- *The Secret Garden*: R. Rubinova’s 1914 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Таинственный_сад_(Бёрнетт)); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 17396](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17396).
- *The Time Machine*: N. A. Morozov’s 1909 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Машина_времени_(Уэллс;_Морозов)); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 35](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/35).
- *The Call of the Wild*: Z. A. Vershinina’s 1928 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Зов_предков_(Лондон;_Вершинина)); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 215](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/215).
- *The Sea-Wolf*: Z. A. Vershinina’s 1929 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Морской_волк_(Лондон;_Вершинина)); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 1074](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1074).
- *The War of the Worlds*: Emilia Pimenova’s 1927 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Борьба_миров_(Уэллс;_Пименова)); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 36](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36).
- *A Little Princess*: the complete 19-chapter text of A. N. Rozhdestvenskaya’s 1908 Russian translation from [Lib.ru’s classical archive](http://az.lib.ru/b/bernett_f_e/text_1905_a_little_princess.shtml); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 146](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/146).
- *Robinson Crusoe*: Maria Shishmareva’s 1928 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Робинзон_Крузо_(Дефо;_Шишмарёва)); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 521](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/521). The source has no digital chapter headings, so the app reconstructs its reading divisions from the English edition.
- *Treasure Island*: Emilia Pimenova’s 1916 Russian translation from [Russian Wikisource](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Остров_сокровищ_(Стивенсон;_Пименова)/ДО); English text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 120](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/120). The app modernizes the principal pre-reform Russian letters and terminal hard signs for readability.

## German editions

- *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland*: Antonie Zimmermann’s 1869 German translation from [Project Gutenberg ebook 19778](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/19778); English text from [ebook 11](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11).
- *Treasure Island*: 1922 German edition from [Project Gutenberg ebook 49424](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/49424); English text from [ebook 120](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/120).
- *Faust, Part One*: Goethe’s German text from [Project Gutenberg ebook 2229](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2229); Bayard Taylor’s 1870–71 English translation from [ebook 14591](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14591).

Project Gutenberg explains that acknowledgements and reference links do not associate an adapted text with its trademark. Russian Wikisource marks the selected translations as public domain and requests attribution through a link to the reused page. Copyright status can vary by country; these editions were chosen from authors and translators whose stated publication and death dates place them outside ordinary life-plus-70-year terms.
